Lubrication Management
Effective Date: October 2026

About This Policy

Pulsarlube is an industrial lubrication-equipment management application used to register, monitor, and control gateway and Bluetooth lubricator devices within your organization. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

The application may collect:

  • Account and profile information (name, email address, and your role/company assignment within your organization)
  • Authentication data (your password, and one-time verification codes used during password resets)
  • Device registration data (gateway and lubricator device names, MAC addresses, and their assignment within your organization's group/location hierarchy)
  • Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) identifiers used to pair with your organization's registered devices
  • Equipment data reported by connected devices (grease fill levels, discharge history, signal strength, and alert/status information)
  • Push notification tokens, used to deliver device alerts
  • Your selected language/locale
  • Customer support inquiry content, when submitted through the app

Account Creation

Pulsarlube accounts are created and provisioned by your organization's administrator. The app does not offer self-service sign-up — if you don't yet have an account, your organization's administrator can create one for you.

How We Use Information

Information is used solely for:

  • Authenticating you and managing your account
  • Connecting to and controlling your organization's registered devices
  • Delivering real-time equipment status and alert notifications
  • Organizing devices within your organization's own group/location structure
  • Improving app reliability and performance
  • Providing customer support

Data Sharing

Pulsarlube does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Device data and commands are transmitted to our backend servers solely to provide app functionality. Push notifications are delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google). The app does not include any advertising or analytics software, and no data is shared with advertising or analytics services.

Data Security

All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS. Account credentials are stored using your device's own secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). Where biometric unlock (Face ID / fingerprint) is enabled, verification is performed entirely on your device by its operating system — Pulsarlube never receives or stores your biometric data. BLE communication with your registered devices uses the device manufacturer's own pairing protocol.

Data Retention

Account and device data is retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see our Account & Data Deletion page for how.

Children's Privacy

Pulsarlube is a business tool for managing industrial lubrication equipment and is not directed at or intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
